Our little

love letter to bread.

Every loaf has a story. Here's ours.

Hi, I'm Joshua — and this is my tiny-kitchen bakery.

The Loafy Dough is a one-baker cottage bakery, run out of a sunny home kitchen. What began as a quiet pandemic-era hobby slowly became something my neighbors couldn't stop asking about.

So here we are. Each week I bake what I love eating: long-fermented sourdough loaves and the gooiest organic cinnamon rolls. Everything is made by hand, scored by hand, and packed up with a little note.

Thank you for being part of this. truly.

♥
How we got here

A short, crumb-by-crumb history.

2020

The starter is born

A jar of flour and water on a windowsill. We named her Pearl. She is older than my cat.

2021

The first loaves go to neighbors

Hand-delivered in paper bags. The thank-you notes that came back changed everything.

2022

Cinnamon rolls join the menu

A snowed-in Saturday experiment. They sold out within an hour. We have not stopped baking them since.

2023

The cottage license comes through

Officially official. The kitchen got a fresh coat of paint and a brand new dough hook.

2024

You found us ♥

Maybe today, maybe next week. Every order still feels like a small miracle. Thank you for being here.

What we believe in

Three things we never skip.

Organic, always

Stone-milled organic flour, real butter, fresh eggs from the farm down the road. The boring kind of integrity.

Slow time

Every dough rests 24–48 hours before it sees the oven. You can taste patience. We promise.

Made by one pair of hands

Every loaf is shaped by me, scored by me, and wrapped by me. Small batches by design.

Questions, sweetly answered

The FAQ corner.

When does the order window open and close?

The menu drops Monday at 9am, and orders stay open until Thursday at midnight (or until we sell out).

Where do I pick up?

At the market! We bring your preorder to whichever market you chose — Arbor Park (Sat), Riverside (Sun), or Downtown Makers (first Fri).

How do I pay?

Venmo or cash at the market. We'll send a friendly reminder with your confirmation.

How should I store my bread?

Sourdough keeps beautifully on the counter, cut-side down, for 3–4 days. Cinnamon rolls reheat best at 325°F for 6 minutes.

Can I order in bulk for a special occasion?

Yes! Reach out at least two weeks ahead. We've done baby showers, brunches, dinner parties, and one very memorable engagement.
