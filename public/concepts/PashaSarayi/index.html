Our story

Where Ottoman heritage meets modern craft

Pasha Sarayi is a culinary brand that blends the rich traditions of Turkish cuisine with contemporary global flavors and techniques. Drawing inspiration from every region of Turkey — from the aromatic spices of the East to the hearty grilled meats of the West — we craft a dining experience unlike any other in Dubai.

Beyond the food, we offer a curated shisha experience and warm hospitality that makes every visit feel like a royal gathering in an Ottoman palace.
